title: Language and the predictive brain
---

As you read or listen, your brain is constantly predicting what comes next, much like a language model. But what exactly is the brain predicting? Words? Syntax? Meaning? And is comprehension just weighing probabilities across thousands of alternatives — like in AI models — or does the brain also commit to specific interpretations, like in symbolic parsers? We use computational modelling combined with eye tracking and neuroimaging (fMRI, MEG) to find out.

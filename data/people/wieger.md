I study how the brain builds predictive models of the visual world, using deep neural networks and neuroimaging to characterise what, when, and how it predicts. I'm a PhD candidate at the Donders Centre for Cognitive Neuroimaging, co-supervised by Floris de Lange and Micha Heilbron.
